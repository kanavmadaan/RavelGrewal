COMMON QUESTIONS

Before we work together.

Will you work with our existing clients?

The relationship structure, communication channels, client authorizations and any non-solicitation terms should be agreed in writing before an engagement begins.

Can we discuss a short-term or recurring engagement?

Yes, inquire about seasonal, project-based or ongoing support. Availability and scope are confirmed in discussion.

Do you provide consultancy for accounting practices?

Practice consulting is an area for discussion, including workflow, systems and capacity planning. A proposed engagement is subject to fit and capability.

How should we share confidential documents?

Do not send documents or client identifiers through this inquiry form. Appropriate secure channels and necessary permissions should be agreed first.
