COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you get started.

What documents should I prepare?

Depending on your situation, gather income slips, prior returns, and records of relevant expenses and deductions. We will confirm what is required for your return.

Can you help if I have not filed for previous years?

Contact us to discuss which years are outstanding and what records are available.

Do you work with clients outside Ontario?

Yes. Ravel has served clients across Canada; confirm the scope of service and any province-specific requirements during your consultation.

How much will my return cost?

Fees depend on the complexity and scope of the work. Discuss your situation before proceeding.

Services and fees are confirmed after discussing your circumstances. Please do not email sensitive tax documents or identification numbers through an initial inquiry.
